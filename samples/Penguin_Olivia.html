Olivia Hagedoorn

Introduction

The Palmer Penguins dataset contains measurements of penguins observed near Palmer Station in Antarctica. It includes three species: Adelie, Chinstrap, and Gentoo, along with information about the islands where they were observed, their sex, and physical measurements such as bill length, bill depth, flipper length, and body mass. The goal of this analysis is to explore how physical measurements differ between male and female penguins and among the three species. I will also examine which species are found on each island and use statistical tests to evaluate differences in selected measurements.

Final Sex - Part A

sex_data <- penguins %>%
  filter(!is.na(sex), !is.na(body_mass_g))

sex_test <- t.test(body_mass_g ~ sex, data = sex_data)
sex_p <- sex_test$p.value

FINALsex <- ggplot(sex_data, aes(x = sex, y = body_mass_g, fill = sex)) +
  geom_boxplot() +
  labs(
    title = "Body Mass by Sex",
    x = "Sex",
    y = "Body mass (g)",
    subtitle = paste("Welch t-test p-value:", signif(sex_p, 3))
  ) +
  scale_fill_brewer(palette = "Set2") +
  theme_minimal() +
  theme(legend.position = "none")

The Welch two-sample t test compared mean body mass between male and female penguins. Male penguins had a higher mean body mass (4,545.7g) than female penguins (3,862.3g). The p value was approximately 4.79 × 10⁻¹⁶, which is below 0.05. Therefore, the results provide strong evidence of a statistically significant difference in mean body mass between the sexes. ### Final Species — Part B

species_data <- penguins %>%
  filter(!is.na(species), !is.na(bill_length_mm))

species_test <- aov(bill_length_mm ~ species, data = species_data)
species_summary <- summary(species_test)
species_p <- species_summary[[1]][["Pr(>F)"]][1]

FINALspecies <- ggplot(
  species_data,
  aes(x = species, y = bill_length_mm, fill = species)
) +
  geom_boxplot() +
  labs(
    title = "Bill Length by Species",
    x = "Species",
    y = "Bill length (mm)",
    subtitle = paste("ANOVA p-value:", signif(species_p, 3))
  ) +
  scale_fill_brewer(palette = "Set2") +
  theme_minimal() +
  theme(legend.position = "none")

FINALspecies

species_summary
##              Df Sum Sq Mean Sq F value Pr(>F)    
## species       2   7194    3597   410.6 <2e-16 ***
## Residuals   339   2970       9                   
## ---
## Signif. codes:  0 '***' 0.001 '**' 0.01 '*' 0.05 '.' 0.1 ' ' 1

A one way ANOVA tested whether mean bill length differed among Adelie, Chinstrap, and Gentoo penguins. The test produced an F statistic of 410.6 and a p-value below 2×10⁻¹⁶. Because the p value is below 0.05, the results provide strong evidence that mean bill length differs among the three species. The ANOVA does not identify which specific species differ from one another.
